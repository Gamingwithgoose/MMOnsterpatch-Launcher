MMOnsterpatch v0.17.1.6 OldRepoPvPFlowRollbackHotfix

Purpose:
- Restore the old working PvP battle rhythm from the public MMOnsterpatch repo instead of forcing the new deterministic/token turn scheduler.
- Fix the observed behavior where player one can attack but the remote/player-two side never receives usable local battle control afterward.

Client changes:
- Removed the forced PvP GetNextBattler deterministic turn-order Harmony patch added in v0.17.1.5.
- Removed the forced InitBattle initial-turn alignment Harmony patch added in v0.17.1.5.
- Kept the old repo core rhythm:
  - Local/player-side turns run vanilla BattleSystem logic.
  - Opponent/enemy-side turns are network-driven and wait for BATTLE_CMD/BATTLE_HIT/BATTLE_DONE.
  - Local move selections send BATTLE_CMD.
  - Local vanilla damage sends authoritative BATTLE_HIT packets.
  - Move completion sends BATTLE_DONE.
  - Remote side replays authoritative hit packets instead of calculating random AI damage.
- Kept the v0.17.1.5 auto-target BATTLE_CMD helper for moves that do not open the manual SelectTarget menu, because this preserves the old command/hit/done model while avoiding missing-command lockups on non-manual-target moves.

Preserved:
- v0.17.1.1 Trading Post save-session hotfix.
- v0.17.1.2 server-truth save pipeline.
- v0.17.1.3 save-select appearance metadata fix.
- v0.17.1.4 remote avatar stale tolerance increase.
- v0.17.1.5 authoritative hit replay packet path.
- Ranked PvP packet/session/result foundation from v0.17.1.
- Client/Launcher/Server folder layout.
- CLEAN_AND_BUILD_ALL.bat and Clean_And_Build_All.ps1 entrypoints.

Not changed:
- No hook/water version-exclusive spawn work.
- No ranked RP performance/remaining-MoN formula.
- No Trading Post reconnect/lazy socket branch.
- No save pipeline rewrite beyond existing v0.17.1.2 behavior.

Testing focus:
1. Test normal PvP first, not ranked.
2. Player one selects a targeted attack.
3. Confirm player two sees exact damage and then receives local battle input.
4. Player two selects a targeted attack.
5. Confirm player one sees exact damage and then receives local battle input.
6. Repeat with a no/manual-target move.
7. Confirm BATTLE_CMD, BATTLE_HIT, and BATTLE_DONE continue alternating in client/server logs.
